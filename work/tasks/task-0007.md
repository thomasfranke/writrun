# Record approval on the merge

**References:** [product/stage-2-pull-requests/approval.md](../../docs/product/stage-2-pull-requests/approval.md) · [spec-0004](../specs/spec-0004.md)

`writrun approve` listens for an approving review, an event this
repository can never produce: its pull requests are authored by the
maintainer, and no forge lets a person approve their own. The gate is
unsatisfiable, so it is being worked around by hand — specs sit `draft`
while their work is done and the field gets typed off the record.

Move the trigger to the merge, which the maintainer performs anyway and
the forge does allow, and record the flip on `main`.

Priority is high because every authoring change pays this toll until it
lands, and each toll is paid by writing an approval outside the machinery
that is supposed to witness it.
